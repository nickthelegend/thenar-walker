Connect the controller

PC with the USB dongle
  1. Plug the receiver in, switch the controller on.
  2. Press any button here. The chip at the top turns green.
PC or phone over Bluetooth
  1. Put the controller in pairing mode (see its manual — usually HOME + a mode button until the LED flashes fast).
  2. Pair it in the system Bluetooth settings.
  3. Open this page in Chrome or Edge and press any button.
No controller? Keyboard works too: WASD drive · arrows right stick · IKJL d-pad · Y/H wrist · Q/E gripper · Z/C speed · B stop · X fine · hold Enter arm on · hold Backspace limp.
